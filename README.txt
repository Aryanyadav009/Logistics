ENTRAX GROUP — Global Trade Website

WhatsApp request flow:
- Buyer, Seller and Meeting forms prepare a WhatsApp message.
- The message opens to ENTRAX GROUP at +91 8858310597.
- Visitor reviews the pre-filled message and presses Send.
- Meeting dates are future-only.
- Monday–Saturday meeting time is limited to 6:00 PM–9:00 PM.
- Sunday meeting time is flexible/by confirmation.

No backend is required for this WhatsApp click-to-chat flow.

Important:
- Uploaded seller documents are NOT transmitted through the WhatsApp link; only their filenames are included in the prepared message.
- For automatic server-side WhatsApp notifications without the visitor pressing Send, a WhatsApp Business Platform/API backend is required.

Open index.html in a modern browser or deploy the folder to a static hosting service.
